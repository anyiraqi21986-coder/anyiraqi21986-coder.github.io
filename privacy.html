Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Mudhafer Auto Publisher is a personal desktop application used by the account owner to publish original video files to the owner’s authorized YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Information processed

The application processes video files, titles, descriptions, publishing preferences, platform identifiers, and OAuth authorization tokens needed to perform publishing requested by the user.

Use and storage

Information is used only to authenticate the user, upload selected content, check publishing results, refresh authorization when permitted, and prevent duplicate uploads. Settings, publishing history, and authorization tokens are stored locally on the user’s computer. The operator does not run a separate data-collection server.

Third parties

Content and related metadata are transmitted only to Google/YouTube and TikTok when required to complete the user’s request. Information is not sold or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

The user may delete the application’s local data folder or revoke access through the connected Google and TikTok account settings.

Security

The application uses OAuth and does not request or store platform passwords. Users are responsible for securing their computer and application credentials.

Children

The application is not directed to children and is intended for use by an adult account owner.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to the application owner through the TikTok account used to authorize this application.
